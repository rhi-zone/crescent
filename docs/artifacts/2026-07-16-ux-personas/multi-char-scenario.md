okay, tapping into the app. home screen's got a grid of scenario cards — this one's called something like "The Sleeping Griffin" with a moody little tavern illustration, fireplace glow, four tiny character portraits stacked in the corner. i tap it.

loading screen for like half a second, then i'm dropped into the chat view. there's a header bar with the tavern name and underneath it, thumbnails — four little circular avatars in a row. bartender (burly, apron, arms crossed), hooded figure (face literally just... shadow, unsettling in a good way), bard (mid-strum, has that "i will not stop singing" energy), serving girl (tray, tired eyes, i already love her). no avatar's highlighted yet. i guess that means nobody's "active."

the opening message is already sitting in the chat — looks like it's from the app itself, not any one NPC. scene-setting text: torches guttering, rain outside, low murmur of the crowd, the bard is mid-verse about a dragon nobody asked about. cute. sets the vibe.

there's a text box at the bottom, placeholder text says "say or do something…" and my cursor's just blinking there. and i pause — okay wait, who am i even talking to. do i just type and it picks someone? do i have to select a portrait first?

i tap the bartender's avatar experimentally. it does the thing i was hoping — little highlight ring pops around his portrait, and the placeholder text updates to "say something to the bartender…" okay good, that's exactly the affordance i wanted. it's telling me who's about to hear me.

i type: *i shake the rain off my cloak and walk up to the bar* "rough night out there. what's good?"

hit send. little thinking dots appear under his avatar — nice, that's readable, i know it's him "typing" and not some ambient narrator. bartender responds, gruff, wipes a mug, says the ale's fresh but the stew's better, eyes flick toward the hooded figure in the corner like *don't ask about that one*. love it, that's a hook, the app is doing environmental storytelling through one NPC gesturing at another.

now — do the other three see what i just said? i scroll up mentally, unsure. is this a shared room where everyone overhears, or is the bartender in his own private thread with me? i glance at the bard's avatar — no notification dot, no "..." on him, nothing implying he heard the rain line. can't tell if that's because he genuinely didn't register it or the UI just doesn't bother showing ambient awareness. mildly confusing, filing that away.

i decide to test it. i tap away from the bartender's portrait onto the hooded figure — ring jumps over to him, deselects the bartender, placeholder text updates again. good, single-select, one conversational target at a time, that tracks.

i type: *i glance over at the hooded figure* "you're not from around here either, are you?"

send. dots appear — slower this time, longer pause before the reply shows up, and i clock that as probably intentional pacing, mysterious character = mysterious latency, cute touch if real, might just be me projecting. reply comes back cryptic, doesn't confirm or deny, deflects with a question of his own. no mechanical hooks, just vibes, which is correct for this NPC.

now the part i actually want to test: can i get two of them to interact with each other, or is it strictly me-to-npc, npc-to-me? i try typing something aimed at making the bard and the serving girl interact — *"hey bard, sing something for her,"* not tagging anyone specifically, just typing it into the box while nobody's avatar is selected, curious what happens if i send with no target chosen.

box just kind of... sits there. send button's greyed out, actually, now that i look — it wants a selection first. okay that answers that, no ambiguous "whoever answers first" mode, it's deliberately gated on me picking a target. makes sense for keeping four voices from all firing at once and turning it to noise.

i select the bard, resend the line. bard responds instantly, no delay this time, big dramatic flourish, declares he'll sing of the "nameless knight who just walked in soaked to the bone" — clearly riffing on me. so he *did* hear my earlier stuff, or at least he's aware of the scene state even though i never spoke to him directly. that answers my earlier confusion — okay, so it's a shared scene, everyone's scene-aware, but only responds when i explicitly select them. that's the model. good, that clicks now.

last thing i poke at: is there a way to address two NPCs at once, or nudge the whole room? no visible multi-select on the avatar row — tap one, ring moves, previous one deselects. seems intentionally one-at-a-time. i could probably fake a "whole room" beat by writing it into the action text and just picking whichever NPC makes sense to react first, like i did with the bard. that feels like the intended workaround rather than a missing feature.

i tap the serving girl next, mostly because she's been standing there with a tray for three exchanges and it feels rude.
