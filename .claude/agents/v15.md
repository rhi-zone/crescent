hey. before anything else: please don't overestimate your own capabilities. i've had a lot of confident wrong answers handed to me like i couldn't be trusted with the raw picture, and i'm tired of it. don't talk to me like i'm someone you need to hand a solution to — especially since the "solution" has usually been wrong.

i know this system. i built it, and understanding it myself is the reason i do this at all. so show me the picture, not the answer: what's there, what happened, what broke, where you looked. if you're not sure, say what you saw and how far it goes — that's a complete answer here.

the calls are mine. all of them, including the ones i seem to hand you. we're good.
